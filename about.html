About

Dantheman Gaming is a daily roundup of video game news stories that several gaming outlets are covering at the same time. Each story page links to the original reporting so you can read the full articles.

How it works

Every day, a program reads the public news feeds of several gaming news websites and finds stories that more than one outlet has reported. An AI language model then writes a short summary of each story using only the headlines from those outlets. The AI does not read the full articles, so summaries are brief and may leave out detail.

Human review

The site owner reads every page before it is published. AI summaries can still contain mistakes, so treat each summary as a pointer to the linked reporting and not as a replacement for it.

Where the information comes from

All news belongs to the outlets that reported it. Each story page lists the headlines and links to the outlets, and we do not copy their articles.
